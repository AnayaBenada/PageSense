PageSense

Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

In short: PageSense works entirely on your own computer. The pages you read, the questions you ask and the pages you save are never sent to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

What PageSense reads

When you open the PageSense side panel and ask a question, use Smart Find, or save a page, the extension reads the text of the web page in your current tab. It does this only when you use one of these features, and only for that tab.

Where that data goes

The page text and your question are sent to the PageSense helper app running on your own computer (at http://localhost:8000 by default). That app runs the AI models locally and sends the answer back to the extension. Nothing is sent to the PageSense developers or to any other server.

What is stored, and where

  • Your chat with each page is kept in Chrome's session storage on your computer and is erased when you close Chrome.
  • Pages you save to your library are stored in a file on your computer (~/.pagesense/library.db) until you remove them in the panel or delete the file.
  • The helper app's address (a setting) is saved with Chrome's sync storage, so Chrome may sync it to your other computers if you're signed in to Chrome. No page content is stored there.
  • A short-term cache of recently read pages is kept in the helper app's memory and is cleared when it stops.

Network connections

The extension connects only to the helper app on your computer, and opens the PageSense install page if the helper app isn't installed yet. The installer downloads the AI models once, from Ollama and Hugging Face. Afterwards, the helper app may check Hugging Face for updated model files when it starts; these checks contain no page content, questions or personal information.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, sell or share any personal data or browsing data.
  • We don't use your data for advertising, credit decisions or any purpose other than the features you use.
  • We don't use analytics, tracking or third-party services in the extension.

Permissions

  • Read and change data on websites: to read the text of the page you ask about, highlight passages on it, and talk to the helper app on your computer.
  • Side panel: to show PageSense beside the page.
  • Storage: to keep your chat and settings as described above.
  • Scripting: to read the page's text and highlight passages when you ask.

Deleting your data

Remove saved pages from the library in the panel, or delete everything with ~/PageSense/uninstall.sh --all. Uninstalling the extension removes its chats and settings from Chrome.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? Open an issue at github.com/AnayaBenada/PageSense/issues.
